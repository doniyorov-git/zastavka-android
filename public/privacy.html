1. Who we are

Zastavka (“we”, “us”, “our”) operates a wallpaper store on the web and in mobile apps. You can browse the catalog, keep a personal library of saved images, and unlock premium wallpapers with coins. The Service is offered in English, Russian, and Chinese.

  • Website: https://silkrood.uz/zastavka/
  • iOS and Android apps published as Zastavka
  • Application package / bundle ID: com.zastavka.app

This policy applies to people who browse the catalog, create an account, save or unlock wallpapers, or top up coins. The admin panel is an internal tool and is not offered to end users. By using Zastavka, you agree to the practices described here. If you do not agree, do not create an account or use paid features.

Related documents: Terms of Use and account deletion.

2. Information we collect

We collect information you give us, information created when you use the Service, and information needed to complete a coin top-up. We only collect what we need to run a wallpaper store.

Account information you provide

  • Name
  • Email address
  • Password (stored only as a one-way hash; we cannot read the original password)

App and website activity

  • Saved / favorite wallpaper IDs
  • Unlocked wallpaper IDs and purchase history (wallpaper, price in coins, time)
  • Coin balance
  • Coin top-up records: amount, currency, status, time, and a payment session or order id

Technical information

  • A session token stored on your device after you sign in
  • Language preference stored on your device (English, Russian, or Chinese)
  • Short-lived server logs (IP address, date, requested URL, user-agent) used to keep the Service running and to investigate abuse

Payment-related information

Coin top-up opens Buy Me a Coffee. We send your name, email, and the order amount so the payment can be completed. Card numbers, crypto wallet keys, and other payment-instrument details are entered on the provider’s page. We do not store full card numbers, CVV, or wallet seeds on Zastavka servers.

Information we do not collect

  • Precise location or GPS
  • Your contacts, or photos from your library (except a wallpaper file you choose to save or share)
  • Advertising IDs for ads — we do not run third-party advertising SDKs
  • Health data, financial-account logins, or government IDs

3. How we use your information

We use personal information to provide and improve the Service. That includes using it to:

  • Create your account and authenticate sign-in
  • Show the catalog, keep favorites, unlock wallpapers, and maintain your coin balance
  • Start a checkout session and credit coins after payment is confirmed
  • Prevent fraud, abuse, and unauthorized access
  • Comply with law, including tax or dispute records where required
  • Answer support, privacy, or deletion requests

We do not use your data to build advertising profiles. We do not sell personal data. We do not use the data for tracking across other companies’ apps or websites.

Legal bases (where GDPR / UK GDPR applies)

  • Contract: account, catalog, purchases, coin balance
  • Legitimate interests: security logs, abuse prevention, service reliability
  • Consent: optional device features such as sharing a downloaded wallpaper through the system share sheet
  • Legal obligation: records we must keep under applicable law

4. Saved wallpapers, coins, and unlocks

When you save a wallpaper, unlock an image, or hold coins, we store those records against your account so the library follows you across the website and the apps. That content exists so you can reopen favorites, keep access to images you unlocked, and spend coins inside Zastavka.

Unlocked wallpapers are licensed for personal use on devices you own or control. Saving or unlocking an image does not transfer ownership of the artwork to you. We and our licensors keep the intellectual property in the catalog.

Coins are an in-Service balance. They are not cash, cryptocurrency, or a stored-value instrument you can withdraw. Details of how coins work are in the Terms of Use.

5. Sharing your information

We share personal information only when it is needed to run the Service:

  • Buy Me a Coffee: name, email, order amount, and the order id in the coffee message so a coin top-up can be matched. Card and other payment-instrument data stays with Buy Me a Coffee.
  • Hosting: the app, website, and database run on our cloud server. The host processes data only to store and deliver the Service.
  • Authorities: if required by law, or to protect users from fraud or harm.

We do not share data with advertisers, data brokers, or social networks for their marketing. We do not sell personal information. We do not sell wallpaper files from your library.

6. Payments

Payments for coin packs are processed through Buy Me a Coffee (buymeacoffee.com/admin09wet). When you tap Pay, Zastavka opens that Buy Me a Coffee checkout (in the system browser on iOS and Android). Buy Me a Coffee may collect and process payment details under its own privacy and security policy, which you should read on that page before you pay.

Zastavka does not store full credit or debit card numbers. We keep a limited order record (amount, currency, status, time, and an order id) so we can credit coins after a signed Buy Me a Coffee webhook and handle disputes.

7. Cookies, local storage, and similar technologies

Zastavka is a wallpaper store, not an advertising network. We use essential storage on your device so the Service works. We do not use advertising cookies, retargeting pixels, or third-party analytics cookies.

On your device we may store:

  • Session token — keeps you signed in until you log out or we revoke the session
  • Language — remembers English, Russian, or Chinese

These values typically live in local storage (or an equivalent app store on iOS/Android), not in advertising cookies. The Buy Me a Coffee checkout page may set its own cookies; those are controlled by Buy Me a Coffee, not by Zastavka. Server logs described above are kept on our host for a short time.

You can clear site data in your browser, or log out in Profile, which removes the session we issued. Clearing local storage signs you out and may reset language to the default until you choose again. Deleting the app removes local app data on that device; it does not delete your account on our servers.

8. Communications

We do not run an advertising SDK and we do not send promotional marketing campaigns from Zastavka. We may still send or display service messages that are needed to operate your account — for example, a checkout confirmation from Buy Me a Coffee, or a response to a deletion or support request.

If we ever introduce optional product emails, we will ask for permission where the law requires it, and you will be able to opt out of marketing without losing service notices that are required to run the account.

9. How long we keep information

We keep personal information only as long as reasonably needed for the purposes in this policy.

  • Account, favorites, unlocks, and coin balance: until you delete the account
  • Payment / top-up records: kept with the account and deleted with it, unless a law requires a limited financial record
  • Session token: until you log out, or when we revoke it
  • Server logs: typically up to 30 days, unless needed longer to investigate an incident

After a valid deletion request we delete or irreversibly anonymize account data. We do not “freeze” an account instead of deleting it.

10. Security

We use HTTPS in transit, hashed passwords, and access controls on the server. We take reasonable technical and organizational steps to protect personal information against loss, misuse, unauthorized access, disclosure, alteration, or destruction.

No website, app, or data transmission is completely secure. You are responsible for keeping your password private and for using a device you trust.

11. International transfers

The Service is hosted on a server that may be outside your country. If you use Zastavka from the EU, the UK, or another region, your data is transferred to that host in order to provide the Service. Where required, we take reasonable steps to protect information in line with applicable privacy laws.

12. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights over your personal information, including the right to:

  • Access the data we hold about your account
  • Correct inaccurate name or email
  • Delete your account and associated data
  • Export a copy of account data we hold
  • Object to or restrict certain processing
  • Withdraw consent for optional device features by not using them

You can log out at any time in Profile. You can save or remove favorites in the app or on the website. To make a privacy request, use the account deletion page or the developer contact email shown on the Google Play and App Store listing for Zastavka. We may need to verify that you control the account email before we respond.

13. Account and data deletion

If you created an account, you can request deletion in both of these ways (required for apps that offer account creation):

How to delete
  • In the app: Profile → Delete account. This permanently deletes the signed-in account.
  • On the web (no app install needed): Delete your Zastavka account

What we delete: name, email, password hash, session tokens, favorites, coin balance, wallpaper unlocks tied to the account, and top-up records tied to the account.

What we may retain for a limited time if required by law: a non-identifying payment reference needed for accounting or dispute handling. We aim to complete deletion within 7 days (usually immediately when you confirm in-app or submit the web form with the account email).

Deleting the app from your phone does not delete the account. Use in-app delete or the web page.

14. Children’s privacy

Zastavka is a general wallpaper store. It is not directed at children under 13, or the equivalent minimum age in your country. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child created an account, use the deletion page and we will delete it.

15. Third-party links and services

The Service may open Buy Me a Coffee, system share sheets, or store listings operated by others. We are not responsible for the privacy practices, content, or security of third-party websites or services. You should read their policies before providing information to them.

16. Public content, reviews, and sharing

Zastavka does not publish a public social feed of your saved wallpapers. If you post a review, screenshot, or comment about Zastavka on an app store or another public platform, that content is visible according to that platform’s rules.

If you use the system share sheet to send a wallpaper you already unlocked, the destination app (Messages, Photos, and so on) handles that share under its own policy. We do not scan your camera roll.

17. Legal requirements

We may use or disclose personal information if we believe it is reasonably necessary to:

  • Comply with applicable law or legal process
  • Respond to lawful requests from authorities
  • Protect our rights, customers, property, or the Service
  • Investigate fraud, security issues, abuse, or violations of the Terms of Use
  • Enforce agreements or resolve disputes

18. Device permissions

The mobile apps may use the system share sheet so you can save a wallpaper you already unlocked. Photo library access, if requested by the operating system, is only to store a wallpaper you downloaded. We do not scan your camera roll, read your contacts, or track precise location.

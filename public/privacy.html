Privacy Policy

Last updated: 15 September 2026

Zastavka (“we”) provides a wallpaper store app and website. This policy explains what we collect and why.

Data we collect

  • Account data: name, email, and a hashed password when you register.
  • App activity: saved wallpapers, purchases, and coin balance.

How we use data

We use this information to create your account, deliver wallpapers you buy or save, and keep your coin balance.

Payments

Coin top-ups open a secure payment page. We may send your name, email, and order amount so the purchase can be completed. Card and other payment details are entered on that page and are not stored on our servers. Coins are added to your account after payment is confirmed.

Storage

Account and purchase records are stored on our servers. Session tokens are stored on your device.

Sharing

We do not sell personal data. We do not share it with advertisers.

Your choices

You can log out in Profile. To delete an account, email the developer listed on the Play Store listing.

Contact

Questions: use the Play Store developer contact email.

Delete your Zastavka account

Wallpaper store · package com.zastavka.app

This page is the public web way to request deletion of a Zastavka account and the data linked to it. You can also delete from the app: Profile → Delete account.

What we delete

  • Name, email, hashed password
  • Login sessions
  • Saved wallpapers, unlocks, and coin balance
  • Top-up records tied to the account

Deletion is permanent. We aim to complete it immediately when this form is submitted with the email on the account (within 7 days at most). Deleting the app from your phone does not delete the account.
